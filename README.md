# Mystery Numbers 📦

Aoife's pre-algebra game — live at **https://aoife-algebra.vercel.app**

A letter is just a mystery box wearing a name sticker! 21 gentle levels across six
worlds take her from "what's hiding in the box?" all the way to solving two-step
equations and cracking word problems — with frogs hopping below zero, bags to tip
out and pack up, balance scales, and number machines along the way.

Each level has the same ladder as her other games: **👀 Watch → 🤝 Together →
🧭 Lead → 🦋 Solo**, plus **🔁 Practice** (five in a row). Fresh problems every
time, no timers, nothing ever locked.

Parent peek: tap the title 5 times quickly to see her progress.
